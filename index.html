WHAT WE RUN

Art, Robots, Workshops, and Exhibitions

ArtRoboticsLabs is a creative technology lab and education EVENT inspiring the next generation of inventors, makers, and problem solvers through art, robotics, engineering and innovation. We offer workshops, competitions, exhibitions and STEM programs.

Event

Art & Robotics & Exhibitions

Experience a creative technology event where makers, artists, engineers, and innovators come together to showcase ideas, interactive projects, experimental machines, and inventions built to inspire the future.

Workshops

Create & Experiment & Labs

Get hands-on with technology through guided workshops and build sessions. Explore robotics, electronics, 3D printing, programming, engineering, and creative design from your first idea to a working invention.

Competitions

Invent & Compete & Challenge

Put your creativity and engineering skills to the test. Compete with robots, inventions, and original creations in exciting challenges designed to celebrate innovation, problem-solving, and great ideas.
